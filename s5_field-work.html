5. Preparing Field Work

Step 5 focuses on preparing the field work. This step begins with a detailed onboarding email for the selected survey firm, ensuring they are well-informed and equipped with all necessary resources. The email should also contain an invitation for a kick-off meeting to align everyone’s understanding of the project scope, timeline, and specific roles.

This step ensures that both the Task Team Leader (TTL) and the survey firm are fully prepared to execute the field work efficiently, addressing any potential questions and closely supervising the firm’s preparations to ensure adherence to the GEPD’s project standards.

Step-by-step Guide

5.1. Email for Onboarding the Survey Firm:

The onboarding email to the survey firm marks the beginning of Step 5. It formally welcomes the firm and provides essential information and resources for the project. The email - a sample of it can be downloaded below too - should include attached the following documents:

Resources Available for 5.1:

  • School Survey Instruments: English

  • Manual of the School Survey: English

  • Step-by-step Guide for the School Survey: English

  • Survey of Public Officials Instruments: English

  • Manual of the Survey of Public Officials: English

  • Step-by-step Guide for the Survey of Public Officials: English

  • Recordings of Previous Survey Firm’s Training: Videos

In addition, the email outlines the immediate steps for the firm, which include: - Adapting instruments to the Country of implementation’s reality - Planning on translations of the Instruments - if applicable - Inviting the Firm to a kick-off meeting.

5.2. Kick-off Meeting with the Firm

The kick-off meeting, is the space for the TTL to ensure that the project’s scope, timeline, and roles are all clear for the survey firm. It’s the moment to go through the contract details, including deliverables and quality standards, so addressing initial questions and setting clear expectations are key. The TTL should highlight critical milestones and discuss immediate preparatory steps.

5.3. Introduce the Firm to Survey Solutions

As part of the fieldwork preparation, it’s crucial to integrate the Survey Solutions tool for managing and deploying surveys effectively. This involves granting the survey firm access and providing necessary training on using the platform.

Steps to Integrate Survey Solutions:

  • Granting Access: the survey firm will receive access to the Survey Solutions platform through a headquarters account. This allows them to work with the instrument, manage data collection, but does not grant them the ability to modify the survey design directly.
  • Technical Session: for the TTL this is likely the most relevant part within this sub-step. A technical session is necessary to train the survey firm on using Survey Solutions. This session covers essential topics like user creation, assignment setup, and understanding the approval process. Typically, the firm learns to create users and set up assignments in bulk, manage interviews, and monitor data collection.
  • Piloting Phase: before full deployment, the firm will use Survey Solutions to run a pilot test. This phase is critical for identifying any issues with the survey instrument or data collection process. During piloting, the firm can provide feedback on question clarity and flow, which the team can address before the full rollout. Understanding how to version control works within the platform is key here.
  • Translations and Version Control: survey Solutions allows for easy accommodation of multiple languages through a downloadable template for translation - which after translation needs to be uploaded to the platform. Each country implementation can use its customized version, with the ability to toggle between languages on the platform.

A very useful resource for this step is the Technical Session Manual, generously produced by a GEPD’s member. This guide is an essential resource for the survey firm, helping them familiarize themselves with the tool’s functionalities and ensuring a smoother onboarding process. It includes details on:

  • Accessing the Survey Solutions platform
  • Creating users and assignments
  • Installing Survey Solutions Apps
  • Monitoring data collection
  • Downloading data

5.4. Supervising the Preparations of the Firm

The final step of Preparing the Field Work concerns the TTL responsibility of supervising the survey firm’s preparations, ensuring they meet GEPD standards and adhere to project timelines. This role might involve:

  • Ensuring the Survey Firm have access to all necessary materials
  • Overseeing the Survey Firm’s training process and pre-tests of the tools
  • Overseeing the instrument adaptation, translations and elaboration of the field protocols
  • Addressing the Survey Firm questions about the survey methodologies

The TTL must also confirm that logistical plans for field work are finalized. Regular check-ins are key for assessing the firm’s progress and addressing potential issues promptly.
